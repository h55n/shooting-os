# 21. WHAT PAPA BELIEVES SHOOTING TEACHES

The masterclass source highlights:

## Concentration & Focus
Shooting requires:
- concentration,
- focus,
- control of attention.

Yoga and meditation are discussed as useful training approaches for focus.

## Discipline & Patience
Shooting requires special discipline because:
- it involves firearms,
- safety is essential,
- patience is critical,
- every shot matters.

Papa's teaching idea is that losing patience on even one shot can affect the result and potentially the competition outcome.

## Breathing & Body Control
Papa teaches that:
- breathing affects body balance,
- breathing should be controlled and normal,
- excessive breath-holding or poor breathing can negatively affect shooting,
- body control is important across positions.

## Mental Fitness
Papa describes shooting as a sport in which:
- mental fitness is extremely important,
- stress can affect performance,
- pressure can affect shooting,
- mental training is therefore part of preparation.

## Competition Pressure
Papa places strong emphasis on:
- handling match pressure,
- understanding physical signs of pressure,
- learning to perform under high-pressure conditions.

## Confidence
Confidence is considered essential to shooting performance.

Papa teaches that confidence should be developed through training and practice rather than treated as something automatic.
