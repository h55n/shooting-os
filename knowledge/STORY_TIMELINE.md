## PHASE 2 — CRPF JOINING & EARLY STRUGGLE

**Period:** Early CRPF years  
**Status:** ✅ Source document

Key events:
- Joined CRPF after shooting success.
- Wanted to join the **CRPF Shooting Team**.
- Was initially sent to his company for **General Duty** instead.
- Came to Headquarters for work and discovered a vacancy in the **Sector Shooting Team**.
- After persistent effort, was **selected for the Central Shooting Team CRPF, Neemuch**.
- The team initially had opportunities mainly for **All India Police Shooting (AIPSC)**.
- Sports weapons for some national-level events were not available.

**Story arc:**
> Wanted shooting team → Got General Duty → Found opportunity → Worked for it → Selected

**Content angle:**  
*"Team mein jagah maangne se nahi milti — sirf performance se milti hai."*

## PHASE 4 — EQUIPMENT SHORTAGE & THE INSAS RIFLE

**Period:** During CRPF Neemuch period  
**Status:** ✅ Source document

Key events:
- Papa wanted to compete in **300m Big Bore** but the required rifle was not available.
- The team tried fitting a sight onto an **INSAS rifle**.
- The sight came off **during a match**.
- That attempt did not succeed.
- The team was later moved to **Gurgaon** after the Commonwealth Games for AIPSC preparation.
- CRPF obtained an **SSG .308 rifle on loan**.
- A new DG ordered that the SSG rifle be provided to the team/Papa.
- Papa's results improved — he **won medals that year**.

**Key figure:**  
- **Shri Parvinder Singh** — team manager who tried to create changes and support the shooting team.

**Story arc:**
> No rifle → INSAS experiment → Failed → New management → SSG .308 → Medals

**Content angle:**  
*"Resources kam the, lekin rukna kabhi nahi tha."*

## PHASE 5 — 2012 HYDERABAD BIG BORE NATIONAL

**Period:** 2012  
**Status:** ✅ Source document

Key events:
- The **Big Bore National** was held in **Hyderabad** in 2012.
- **Chandu Sir** (guru) gave Papa his personal sports rifle to compete with.
- Papa competed and achieved **Top 6 position in three events**. ✅
- That year, an **Asian Games Indian team selection** was planned.
- A coaching camp was held at **MHOW / AMU** (Asian Games preparation).
- During the camp, Papa strongly felt the need for his **own sports rifle**.
- This became the trigger to apply for a licence and get a personal rifle.

**Content angle:**  
*"2012 mein borrowed rifle se Top 6 — aur phir apna rifle lene ki zaroorat samajh aayi."*

**Status:** ✅ Source document
