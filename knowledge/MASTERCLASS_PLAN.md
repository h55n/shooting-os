## MODULE STRUCTURE

### MODULE 1 — Shooting Ko Samjhein (Understanding the Sport)

**Goal:** Give beginners a complete picture of the sport before they start.  
**Length estimate:** 1 lesson (~12–15 min video)

**Lessons:**
1. Shooting kya hai — ek sport, ek skill, ek career
2. Rifle, Pistol, Shotgun — teen bade categories kya hain
3. Events kya hain — 10m, 50m, 300m, kab kya istemal hota hai
4. India mein shooting ka scope — kahan jaata hai ek shooter

**Key teaching point:**  
Shooting is not one thing — it's a family of disciplines. Beginners should understand the full map before choosing their path.

---

### MODULE 2 — Beginner Fundamentals

**Goal:** Teach the 10 fundamentals in the correct order.  
**Length estimate:** 8–10 lessons (~6–10 min each)

**Lessons:**
1. Safety — Rules jo kabhi nahi todna
2. Stance — Foundation of everything
3. Grip — Kitna tight, kitna loose, aur kyun
4. Breathing — Natural pause, sahi timing
5. Sight Alignment — Kya dekhna hai aur kahan
6. Sight Picture — Alignment ka practical use
7. Trigger Operation — Squeeze, surprise, follow through
8. Follow Through — Zyaadatar log yahan galti karte hain
9. Dry Practice — Ghar par practice kaise karein
10. Training Structure — Kitna, kab, aur kaise

**Key teaching point:**  
These fundamentals are not suggestions — they are the technical floor. Skipping any one creates a ceiling on performance.

---

### MODULE 3 — Kyun Shooters Improve Nahi Karte

**Goal:** Address the plateau problem honestly. High value for intermediate shooters.  
**Length estimate:** 4–5 lessons (~8–12 min each)

**Lessons:**
1. Practice vs. correct practice — bada fark hai
2. Competition experience ki zaroorat — sirf practice kafi nahi
3. Technical mistakes jo score rok rahe hain — common analysis
4. Mental blocks — pressure ko ignore karna ek mistake hai
5. Training without structure — kyun range time waste ho jaata hai

**Key teaching point:**  
Most shooters practice. Few train correctly. The gap between practice and performance is almost always explained by one of these five reasons.

---

### MODULE 4 — Competition Psychology (Dimag ki Shooting)

**Goal:** Mental performance training. This is Papa's strongest differentiation vs. basic technique coaches.  
**Length estimate:** 5–6 lessons (~8–12 min each)

**Lessons:**
1. Competition pressure kya hai — physical aur mental dono
2. Pre-match routine — kya karna chahiye, kya avoid karein
3. Focus maintain karna — series ke beech mein kya karein
4. Ek kharab shot ke baad reset — practical drill
5. Patience ka training — impatient shots ko kaise rokein
6. Confidence kaise build hoti hai — training se, feel se nahi

**Key teaching point:**  
Mental performance is not a personality trait — it's a skill that can be trained. Papa's 2017 injury + NSCC medal is the anchor story for this module.

---

### MODULE 5 — Shooting Pathway (Kahan Jaana Hai)

**Goal:** Give shooters and parents a realistic long-term roadmap.  
**Length estimate:** 4–5 lessons (~10–15 min each)

**Lessons:**
1. Beginner to Club — pehla stage kya hota hai
2. District to State — selection kaise hoti hai
3. State to National — kya chahiye aur kitna time lagta hai
4. National to International — gap ko samjhein
5. Olympic Pathway — quota, qualification, selection (verify current rules before scripting)
6. Shooting as career — realistic options

**Key teaching point:**  
Every level has specific requirements. Parents and athletes need to understand the progression honestly, not with false promises.

---

### MODULE 6 — Papa ki Kahani (Personal Story)

**Goal:** Humanize the expert. Build emotional authority. Show that the path is hard and possible.  
**Length estimate:** 4–5 stories (~10–20 min each)

**Story episodes:**
1. NCC se CRPF tak — shooting ne career banaya
2. General Duty se Central Shooting Team tak — struggle aur persistence
3. INSAS rifle wali kahani — equipment ki kami aur kya seekha
4. 2012 Hyderabad — borrowed rifle se Top 6, aur phir apna rifle lene ka decision
5. 2017 — haath toot gaya, competition nahi chhoda

> ⚠️ All episodes must be scripted using STORY_TIMELINE.md. Exact verified facts only.

**Key teaching point:**  
The point of each story is not the achievement — it's the lesson embedded in the struggle. The INSAS story is not about a rifle; it's about problem-solving under resource constraints.

---

### MODULE 7 — Aapka Agle Kadam (Personalized Next Step)

**Goal:** Convert masterclass viewers into coaching/assessment clients.  
**Length estimate:** 1 lesson + interactive component

**Content:**
1. Self-assessment — where are you in the progression?
2. Common gaps based on level
3. What a personalized coaching / training plan looks like
4. How to get Papa's assessment

**Key teaching point:**  
The masterclass gives knowledge. A coach gives direction specific to you. This module bridges general education and personalized development.

1. Every lesson should have a clear, single teaching point.
2. Every lesson should end with an actionable step or drill.
3. Every lesson with Papa's personal claim must be verified against STORY_TIMELINE.md.
4. Technical claims about current NRAI/ISSF rules must be verified before scripting.
5. No lesson should simply list facts — every point should explain why it matters.
6. Papa's voice (Hinglish, coaching tone, practical examples) must be preserved in all scripts.
