## PILLAR 5 — MYTHS & HARD TRUTHS

**Goal:** Highest reach potential. Challenges assumptions. Creates engagement.

### Myths to Bust
- ✅ "Practice karo — result aayega" → Nahi. Sahi practice karni padti hai
- ✅ "Talent enough hai" → Talent sirf shuruaat hai
- ✅ "Range time = improvement" → Without structure, range time wastes
- ✅ "Expensive equipment se score badhta hai" → Equipment floor set karta hai, ceiling nahi
- ✅ "Mental game matters only at Olympics" → Har level par matter karta hai

### Hard Truths
- ✅ Competition score aur practice score alag kyun hote hain
- ✅ Consistency ek achhe score se zyada valuable hai
- ✅ Ek galat coach kaafi nuksaan kar sakta hai
- ✅ Injury se career khatam nahi hota — 2017 ki kahani
- ✅ Resources ki kami excuse nahi hai — INSAS rifle wali kahani
